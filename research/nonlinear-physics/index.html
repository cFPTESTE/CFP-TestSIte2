Nonlinear Physics

Nonlinear waves and coherent structures – solitons, discrete breathers and their stability – in lattices, optical systems and Bose–Einstein condensates.

Research on nonlinear dynamics at CFP studies how nonlinearity and discreteness give rise to localised, coherent structures, and how stable these structures are.
