Theoretical physics, from quantum materials to the early Universe

The Centro de Física do Porto (CFP) is a research unit based at the Department of Physics and Astronomy of the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Porto. It is devoted to research in theoretical physics: quantum physics and fields in high energy and condensed matter, gravitation and cosmology.

About the centre

The Centro de Física do Porto is the Porto node of the Centro de Física das Universidades do Minho e do Porto (CF-UM-UP), a research unit of the LaPMET consortium – Laboratory of Physics for Materials and Emerging Technologies – with facilities at the Department of Physics and Astronomy of the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Porto.

Centro de Física das Universidades do Minho e do Porto. Created in 2014 by joining two research units founded in 1994 – the Centro de Física da Universidade do Minho (CFUM) and the Centro de Física do Porto (CFP) – and evaluated by FCT. LaPMET Laboratory of Physics for Materials and Emerging Technologies. Brings together four physics research units – CFP and IFIMUP (University of Porto), CFUM (University of Minho) and CeFEMA (Instituto Superior Técnico) – around quantum materials, energy, communications, photonics and nanotechnology.

Condensed matter theory

Quantum Materials

Electronic, topological and collective phenomena in quantum materials, from graphene and moiré heterostructures to quasiperiodic and strongly correlated low-dimensional systems.

  • 2D and moiré materials
  • Quasiperiodic systems
  • Topological matter
  • Correlated and integrable systems
  • Optical and nonlinear response

Formal high energy theory

Quantum Field Theory & Holography

Non-perturbative quantum field theory: the AdS/CFT correspondence, the conformal and S-matrix bootstrap, gauge theories and lattice methods.

  • AdS/CFT and holography
  • Bootstrap methods
  • Lattice and numerical field theory
  • Solitons and defects

Gravitation & Cosmology

Alternative theories of gravity, black holes and gravitational waves, inflation and the dark sector, and tests of fundamental physics in space.

  • Modified gravity
  • Black holes and gravitational waves
  • Early Universe
  • Fundamental physics in space
