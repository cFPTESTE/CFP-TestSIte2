---
title: A new website for CFP
date: 2026-09-26
summary: The centre has a new home on the web, with profiles for every member, research areas, publications and seminars.
---
The Centro de Física do Porto has a new website. It brings together profiles of all members, the centre's three core research areas, selected publications, projects, and the seminar programme.

Profiles are built from the information each member submitted. Members who have not yet sent their profile, or who want to update it or add a photo, should contact the website team.
