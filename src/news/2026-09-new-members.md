CFP welcomed four new members in 2026.

**Luís Felipe Reis** started his PhD under the supervision of Orfeu Bertolami. He works on gravitation and cosmology, in particular cosmologically coupled and primordial black holes.

**Ricardo Elísio** joined as a PhD student.

**Fernando Cruz** and **Marina Pimentel** are MSc students in Theoretical Physics working on quasiperiodic two-dimensional systems. Fernando is supervised by Eduardo Castro and Bruno Amorim and is a research fellow in the QPI-MoireS project. Marina's work focuses on quasiperiodicity and magnetism.

Welcome to all!
